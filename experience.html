yuh fire.

Three bottles. Three heat levels. Same Trini soul. Whether yuh just want a little tickle on de eggs or a full-blown sweat-through-de-shirt experience — we got de drop for you.

/01 — Original
/01 — Mild

Bright.
Every-meal heat.

Mild · 30K SHU

De gateway. Scotch bonnet for flavor, not punishment. Bright, herbal, balanced. This is de bottle that lives on de breakfast table — eggs, avocado toast, Sunday brunch.

Heat Profile

~30K Scoville

  • Tickle, not torch
  • Builds slowly, fades clean
  • Won't dominate de plate
  • Great for newcomers to real heat
Flavor Notes

Bright · Herbal · Citrus

  • Lead: Trinidad scotch bonnet
  • Mid: roasted garlic + onion
  • Top: lime + shado beni
  • Finish: clean apple cider tang
Pair With

Everyday Eats

  • Scrambled eggs + breakfast
  • Avocado toast (game changer)
  • Grilled fish + rice
  • Roasted vegetables
/02 — Carnival
/02 — Hot

De crowd-
pleaser.

Hot · 120K SHU

De best-seller. Scotch bonnet leads, habanero brings de bright punch behind. This is de bottle for de cookout, de jerk chicken, de pizza, de wing night. Versatile, confident, never boring.

Heat Profile

~120K Scoville

  • Hits up front, lingers
  • Sweat on de forehead level
  • Will wake up de plate
  • De daily driver
Flavor Notes

Fruity · Smoky · Deep

  • Lead: scotch bonnet + habanero
  • Mid: charred peppers + roasted garlic
  • Top: smoky paprika + mustard seed
  • Finish: long, warm, satisfying
Pair With

Cookout Heroes

  • Jerk chicken (mandatory)
  • Tacos al pastor
  • BBQ ribs + brisket
  • Wings, pizza, burgers
/03 — Soca Inferno
/03 — Inferno

Respect
de bottle.

Inferno · 350K SHU

Ghost pepper enters de chat. Scotch bonnet + habanero + ghost = a sauce that doesn't just light yuh up — it tells stories. Use a drop, not a dollop. Wash yuh hands. Don't touch yuh face. Tell yuh friends you survived.

Heat Profile

~350K Scoville

  • Punch first, burn forever
  • Genuine sweat-through-shirt
  • Tears? Possibly. Worth it.
  • For de heat heads only
Flavor Notes

Smoke · Earth · Inferno

  • Lead: ghost pepper (ripened)
  • Mid: charred scotch bonnet
  • Top: black garlic + habanero
  • Finish: long, layered, devastating
Pair With

Brave Plates

  • Curry goat (Trini style)
  • Gumbo + jambalaya
  • Pho broth (3 drops max)
  • Bloody mary mix
