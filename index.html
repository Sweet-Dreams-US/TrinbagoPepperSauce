002 — How We Build De Sauce

Three things.
Done de right way.

01 — Grow  ·  02 — Bottle  ·  03 — Burn

/01

Grow de pepper.

Sourced from Caribbean farms — Trinidad scotch bonnet, Tobago habanero, ghost pepper from de back yard. Picked ripe. Picked when de heat is at its peak. No imports from de other side of de world.

  • Trinidad scotch bonnet (rare cultivar)
  • Tobago habanero (sweet + savage)
  • Ghost pepper (Inferno line only)
  • Shado beni + culantro from de garden
/02

Bottle by hand.

Small batches. Cold pressed. Slow simmered. We bottle when it's ready — not when de calendar says so. Every bottle gets a date stamp and a batch number, because real food deserves real respect.

  • Cold-pressed pepper extract
  • Slow simmered for 6+ hours
  • Bottled in 5oz artisan glass
  • Date + batch stamped, every time
/03

Burn with love.

Heat is not de point — flavor is. We build sauces that punch first, then linger. Mild for de weekend cookout. Hot for de jerk chicken. Inferno for when you want de room to know you not playin'.

  • Three heat tiers, one philosophy
  • Flavor first, fire second
  • No high-fructose, no fake heat
  • Pairs with everything you cook

003 — Inside De Bottle

Eight ingredients.
Zero shortcuts.

01

Scotch Bonnet

De heart of de sauce. Trinidad cultivar — sweet, fruity, dangerous. 100K-350K Scoville.

02

Tobago Habanero

Punchier brother. Adds a bright, citrus-y backbone to balance de bonnet's depth.

03

Shado Beni

Caribbean culantro. Gives de sauce its grassy, herbal Trini signature. You can't fake this.

04

Garlic + Onion

Fresh. Roasted. Never powdered. Builds de savory bottom de heat sits on top of.

05

Lime + Vinegar

Fresh-squeezed island lime, raw apple cider vinegar. Brightness and bite.

06

Mustard Seed

Toasted, ground in-house. Adds de slight tang that makes Trini sauce different from anywhere else.

07

Sea Salt + Cane

Caribbean sea salt, raw cane sugar — tiny pinch. Just enough to round de edges.

08

Time + Patience

Six hours of slow simmer. No microwave shortcuts. De sauce tells you when it's done.
