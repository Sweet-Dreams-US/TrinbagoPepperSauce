Questions

The fine print.

How hot is it?

Real Caribbean pepper-sauce hot — somewhere in the 80–120K Scoville range, depending on the batch. Hot enough to wake up a plate, not so hot you can't taste the food underneath. The shado beni and lime keep it bright instead of just searing.

How long does a bottle last after opening?

Best within 90 days of opening when refrigerated. Sealed bottles last 18 months in a cool dark place. Real food, real shelf life.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Anytime. No fees. Skip a quarter or cancel directly from your account dashboard. We'd rather you pause than feel locked in.

Where do you ship?

Free shipping anywhere in the lower 48. International shipping (Canada, UK, Caribbean, Europe) available at checkout. Currently we don't ship to Australia.

Is it gluten-free / vegan / sugar-free?

Gluten-free: yes. Vegan: yes. Sugar-free: nope — there's a small amount of raw cane sugar (about 1g per teaspoon serving) to round out the heat. Diabetic-friendly portions, but we won't claim zero sugar.

Wholesale or restaurant orders?

Yes — for restaurants, retailers, and gift shops. Email trinbagopeppersauce@gmail.com with what you're thinking. Minimum 24 bottles to start.

What if I don't like it?

Hit us back. We'll refund the bottle. Real recipes aren't for everyone — we respect that.
