Questions

De fine print.

How hot is "Inferno" really?

Around 350,000 Scoville units. For comparison: Sriracha is ~2,500. Tabasco is ~3,000. A standard ghost pepper is ~1 million. We blend ghost pepper with scotch bonnet so you get heat with flavor — not just chemical burn. Use a drop, not a dollop. Wash yuh hands after.

How long do bottles last after opening?

Best within 90 days of opening when refrigerated. Sealed bottles last 18 months in a cool dark place. Real food, real shelf life — like grandma made.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Anytime. No fees, no hassle. Skip a quarter, swap your heat selection, or cancel — all from your account dashboard. Real talk: we'd rather you pause than cancel and regret it.

Where do you ship?

Free shipping anywhere in de lower 48. International shipping available at checkout — Canada, UK, Caribbean, Europe. Currently we don't ship to Australia (working on it).

Are these gluten-free / vegan / sugar-free?

Gluten-free: yes. Vegan: yes. Sugar-free: nope — we use a tiny pinch of raw cane sugar to round out the heat. About 1g per teaspoon serving. Diabetic-friendly portions, but we won't lie and say zero sugar.

Do you do bulk / wholesale?

Yes — for restaurants, retailers, and gift shops. Email wholesale@trinbagopeppersauce.com with what you're thinking. Minimum 24 bottles to start.

What if I get a bottle and don't like it?

Hit us back. We'll send you a different heat level on us — or refund de full bottle. Real recipes ain't for everyone. We respect that.
